About Chigozie Iluno Samuel

Organizations moving to the cloud often face challenges balancing security, governance, scalability, operational efficiency, and regulatory compliance. I help design cloud strategy that is secure by default, automated through Infrastructure as Code, and aligned with Zero Trust principles.

With over seven years of experience spanning cloud engineering, architecture, and cybersecurity, I specialize in enterprise Azure landing zones, security, governance, and Terraform-driven infrastructure automation.

My approach combines strong architecture principles with automation to build secure, production-ready environments. My focus is always on security by design, scalability, and cost efficiency.

Beyond tech engagements, I enjoy sharing practical cloud security knowledge through technical writing, architecture case studies, and mentoring young professionals starting their careers in cloud architecture and cybersecurity.
